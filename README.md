# UI-UX-Experiment5

## Aim:
Present  different UX project scenarios (e.g., designing a health app, an e-commerce site, or a government service portal).



## Algorithm:
1. Understand what users expect from the health web app and note their problems.
2. Check other health apps and observe what they do well and what they do poorly.

Decide the main functions your app will offer and plan how users will move through each step.

Create simple screens and make sure everything is easy to read and navigate.

Let users try the app, watch where they get stuck, and improve the design based on their feedback.
